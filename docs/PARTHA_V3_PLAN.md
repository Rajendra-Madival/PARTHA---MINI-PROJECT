# PARTHA V3: The Arrow's Path — Vision & Scene Plan

> This plan supersedes `docs/PARTHA_LANDING_PAGE_SPEC.md`, `docs/PARTHA_LANDING_PAGE_SPEC_v2.md`, and `docs/PARTHA_SPEC_v2_1_ADDENDUM.md` for all landing-page visuals and interactions.

## THE VISION

One long scroll. Rama shoots a glowing arrow at the top of the page. The arrow follows the scroll down and draws a golden path behind it. The path stops at five glowing waypoints. Each waypoint is an epic scene that also shows one Partha step. At the end the arrow lands on the login gate and the gate opens. Look: bright start, dark epic middle, bright end, using the app's white (#FAF7F0, --c-brand-white) and light mint (#ECFDF5, --c-brand-green) plus gold and deep night blue. Laptops first; phones get a lighter version.

## Scenes

0. Hero (light): title Partha, tagline, subline, a button "Follow the arrow" that scrolls to scene 1. Rama will stand here in a later phase.
1. The Ashram (light). Step: Find your guru.
2. The Bridge of Stones (dark, dusk). Step: Request mentorship.
3. Kurukshetra (dark, night). Step: Discover opportunities.
4. The Dawn Lake (light). Step: Knowledge Hub, plus an alumni line.
5. The Gate (light). Step: Login.
